# Podcast Production Workflow

This repository documents my approach to podcast production, audio editing, voiceover workflow, and AI-assisted audio experimentation.

## Focus Areas

- Podcast recording and editing
- Voiceover production
- Audio quality control
- Dialogue cleanup
- Pro Tools workflow
- Creative direction for spoken-word content
- AI voice tools and audio experimentation
- Listener experience and storytelling quality

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to organize notes, workflows, and production approaches related to audio storytelling and voice-driven content.

My background includes voiceover, podcast production, creative project management, and audio editing. I’m especially interested in the intersection of human performance, voice quality, and emerging AI audio technologies.

## Sample Production Workflow

1. Review project goals and intended audience.
2. Organize source audio and production assets.
3. Edit for pacing, clarity, and natural conversational flow.
4. Clean distracting noise while preserving vocal authenticity.
5. Review emotional tone, timing, breaths, and listener experience.
6. Export final audio based on client or platform specifications.
7. Complete final quality-control listening pass before delivery.
